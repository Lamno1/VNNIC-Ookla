# Conceptual framework

The theoretical mechanism is: better digital connectivity may lower information and search costs, lower communication and coordination costs, and improve market access, potentially supporting enterprise activity. The link from connectivity to enterprise activity is **THEORETICAL — NOT CAUSALLY IDENTIFIED**.

This descriptive sprint identifies neither that mechanism nor a connectivity–enterprise relationship. Four institutionally plausible competing explanations are registered in `counter_hypotheses.csv`: reverse causality/operator selection, common local development shocks, speed-test composition, and formalization or spatial reallocation. Each remains unresolved; none can be dismissed using the univariate descriptive evidence produced here.
