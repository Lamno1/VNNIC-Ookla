# Ookla validation limitations

- Both sources reflect voluntary speed-test participation.
- Tile medians are spatial summaries, not population or firm averages.
- Centroid assignment does not allocate tile area across boundaries.
- Historical Ookla files may be reaggregated.
- Q2 VNNIC is validation-only; April–December comparison has temporal mismatch.
- CH3 cannot be eliminated.
