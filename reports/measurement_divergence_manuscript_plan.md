# Measurement-divergence manuscript plan

The manuscript will be a descriptive measurement study of VNNIC and Ookla fixed-broadband signals across the historical 63-province geography, 2021–2024.

The paper will not include enterprise outcomes, H2 estimation, Decision 2269 evaluation, WBES, PCI, mobile Ookla, or additional raw Ookla processing. Its contribution is the decomposition of agreement into common trend, dispersion, yearly rank, province-specific change, aggregation sensitivity, and composition sensitivity.

Drafting is not authorized until `measurement_divergence_paper_protocol_lock.json` and the protocol audit both PASS.
