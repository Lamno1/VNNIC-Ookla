For the full numerical schema use the generated CSV column names and the manuscript's estimand definitions. Prefix A refers to 2021–2024 comparisons, B to 2023–2024; `main` keeps all months and `xAM` removes April–May 2024 from the VNNIC variant. Directions are encoded explicitly, such as O_minus_V versus V_minus_O. Do not reverse signs when transcribing a row.

The source contains `neigh12`/`ward12` for the earlier Ward grouping and `contig12` for adjacency-constrained Ward blocks. Their labels are not interchangeable. Fixed groupings and bootstrap intervals are sensitivity analyses, not calibrated spatial inference.
