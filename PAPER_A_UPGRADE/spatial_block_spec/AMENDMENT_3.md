# Amendment 3 (3 November 2026, written and hashed before any new computation)

Cause: the advisor and the authors want a firmer reason for the number of blocks than "fixed in advance, about five provinces per block". SPEC.md, AMENDMENT_1.md and AMENDMENT_2.md are unchanged. The primary rule stays contiguous blocks with k = 12 whatever these analyses show: k = 12 was fixed before any computation, and it is not re-chosen after seeing coverage. The analyses only report how defensible 12 is; if they point elsewhere, the manuscript says so.

C1. Dependence range (outcome-independent). For each source, Moran's I of the log speed in each of the four quarters of 2024 and of the four log changes 2021 to 2024 (eight vectors per source) is computed in distance bands of 50 km between projected province centroids (EPSG:32648; 0 to 600 km; binary weights; provinces with no neighbour in a band are skipped), with a 999-permutation reference band (2.5th and 97.5th percentiles). The range is the smallest band at which the median Moran's I over the eight vectors lies inside the reference band and stays inside for all larger bands. Block extent is the median over blocks with at least three provinces of the largest centroid distance within the block, for contiguous blocks k = 8, 10, 12, 15, 20 and for the six regions. A grouping is described as having blocks "at least as large as the dependence range" if its median extent is not smaller than the range. Descriptive; no statistic of the study is used.

C2. Coverage by number of blocks. The matched coverage evaluation of SPEC.md section 5 (spatial_pilot.py engine, spatial_main.py, six configurations, 2,000 datasets, 1,999 draws, same root seed) is repeated with the block groupings: six regions (engine key 6) and contiguous blocks with k = 8, 10, 12, 15, 20 (engine keys 8, 10, 12, 15, 20), plus the province bootstrap. Only the list of groupings in the engine is changed. Reported: empirical coverage of the 95 percent interval for the outside-indicator contrast (outcome "delta") and the change correlation (outcome "L4_log_Q2"), by configuration and pooled, and mean interval width if available. The pooled value for k = 12 is read together with its neighbours: if its coverage is within 0.02 of the best contiguous k for both outcomes, 12 is described as not distinguishable in coverage from the best-performing choice among those run; otherwise the manuscript states which k covers best and that 12 was fixed in advance. As before, the simulation does not certify the L3 or L4 intervals of the study.

C3. Reporting. Both analyses go into Supplementary Note S8 (two short paragraphs and one table), and the rationale in section 4.4 is rewritten to rest on C1 and C2 as they come out. No other statement of the paper is changed by this amendment.
